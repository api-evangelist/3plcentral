For many brands, order fulfillment isn’t just part of the daily grind; it is the daily grind. On one hand, outsourcing ecommerce fulfillment could save you time, and on the other, it could affect your margins in a bad way. Whether to handle warehousing in-house, outsource to third-party logistics (3PL) providers, or adopt a hybrid model is a choice that can significantly impact operational efficiency, customer satisfaction, and, ultimately, the bottom line.
