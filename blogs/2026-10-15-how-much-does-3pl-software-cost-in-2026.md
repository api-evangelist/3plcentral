Last updated October 2026 Ask five WMS vendors what their software costs and you will hear five pricing models: per order, per warehouse, per user, per module, or some mix of all four. Quotes run from a few hundred dollars a month to tens of thousands, which makes it hard to tell a fair price from a padded one.
