Last updated October 2026 What are warehouse operations? Warehouse operations are the day-to-day processes that move goods through a facility: receiving, putaway and storage, picking, packing, shipping, and returns, plus the inventory control that keeps the records accurate. Running them well comes down to three things: the people doing the work, the workflows they follow, and the system that tracks every move.
