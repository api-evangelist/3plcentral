The most common warehouse positions are warehouse associate, forklift operator, picker and packer, inventory clerk, shipping and receiving clerk, and warehouse supervisor. Each one maps to a stage of the warehouse flow, from receiving goods to picking, packing, and shipping orders. Below, we break down what each role does, what it pays in 2026, and where it fits in a growing operation.
